Filter lists (compiled into engine.dat and lists/*.dat)
-------------------------------------------------------
The lists are downloaded on the build day. LICENCES.txt holds the header lines each list carries.

uBlock filters, badware, privacy, unbreak, quick fixes, annoyances, cookie notices, URL tracking
  uBlockOrigin/uAssets, https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uAssets : GPL-3.0.
EasyList, EasyPrivacy, EasyList social, annoyances, newsletters, notifications, chat, AI
  https://easylist.to : GPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0, dual. Used here under the GPL.

  https://gitlab.com/malware-filter : CC0-1.0 (as the projects state; their own pages are the authority).
AdGuard Base, optional, off by default
  https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdguardFilters : GPL-3.0.

  https://pgl.yoyo.org/adservers/ : no licence is published. The list page says: "Feel free to combine this list with
